How do I play?

Guide the paddle to keep the ball in play and break the bricks across ten sectors. Armored bricks take extra hits. In the final sector, destroy the Warden’s moving armor before striking its core.

How do I launch and aim?

Tap New mission. Slide one finger on the control strip below the paddle, then lift your finger to launch, or tap Launch. Continue moving along the strip to steer. Near-center paddle hits send the ball upward; edge hits give a sharper angle. Your thumb can stay below the playfield.

How do lives and power-ups work?

You begin with three lives. With multiball, losing a ball costs a life only when it was the last ball in play.

  • Multiball: up to three balls at once.
  • Wide paddle: a wider paddle for 15 seconds.
  • Piercing ball: a piercing effect for 9 seconds.

Catching the same temporary power-up refreshes its duration rather than stacking time. Effects reset when you lose a life or change sectors.

How do I pause and resume?

Tap Pause during a flight, then Resume when ready. Leaving the app, an audio interruption or a disconnected audio route also pauses the game. It does not continue behind the pause screen or in the background.

Relaunching the app returns to the menu, without restoring an unfinished flight or the ball position. Sector select lets you begin a fresh flight in an unlocked sector, with zero points and three lives.

I cannot hear the game or feel haptics

Check Music and Sound effects separately in Settings, your device volume, Silent Mode and the active speaker or headphones. The app uses ambient audio and respects Silent Mode; it does not require microphone permission.

Haptics are optional and available only on supported hardware. Many iPads do not support them.

Language and accessibility

Choose System, Français or English in Settings. The change is immediate and saved. Menus support VoiceOver and Dynamic Type for larger text; effects respect Reduce Motion. Real-time play requires visual tracking and precise movement.

Where are my records and progress?

Your personal best, up to five flight records and unlocked sectors stay on your device. Each record contains a score, sector number and completion flag. There is no account, online leaderboard or cross-device sync. Device backups and restoration are managed by Apple and your device settings, not by an in-game service.

How do I erase local data?

Settings → Erase local data clears scores, flight records and progress and ends any active flight, while keeping your language and audio settings. Deleting the app removes its local data and settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Billing and refunds

Apple handles billing and refund requests for App Store transactions. Visit Apple’s purchase support or request a refund from Apple. The game has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or advertising.

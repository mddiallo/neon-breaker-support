Privacy policy

An offline game. No app data sent off your device.

Effective date: 26 September 2026.

Who publishes the game

Publisher: Mouhamadou DIALLO.
Privacy and support contact:
diouma.diallo@outlook.com

Data collected by the app

Neon Breaker: Station 88, called Neon Breaker in the app, does not collect or transmit data off your device to the publisher or a third party. The game has no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDK, server or network connection. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera or advertising identifier.

Data kept on your device

The game stores your chosen language, audio and haptic settings, personal best, up to five flight records, unlocked sectors and campaign completion locally in its private iOS/iPadOS storage. Records contain only a score, sector number and completion flag. They are not uploaded. The current ball position and an unfinished flight are not saved for relaunch. There is no cross-device sync.

Your controls

Settings → Erase local data removes scores, flight records and progress and ends any current flight. It keeps your language and audio choices. Deleting the app removes its local data and settings. Device backups may include app data; backup and restore behavior is controlled by your Apple device and account settings.

Apple services

Apple manages App Store transactions, receipts, billing and refund requests, as well as device backups and any platform diagnostics you choose to share with Apple. Those activities are governed by Apple’s privacy policy. The game has no in-app purchases or subscriptions and does not integrate its own purchase service, telemetry, crash reporter or cloud sync.

If you contact support

If you voluntarily send an email, the publisher receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to and handle your request. Do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Support correspondence is kept only for as long as necessary to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact diouma.diallo@outlook.com to request access to or deletion of that correspondence where applicable.

This website and GitHub Pages

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. These hosting and security logs are separate from the offline game. The game does not send data to this website.

These pages themselves add no cookies, analytics, trackers, advertising, forms, external fonts or client-side scripts. GitHub handles hosting and security logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Refer to that statement for GitHub’s processing and retention practices.

About this policy

The game does not request personal information from any player, including children. This policy covers Neon Breaker and these support pages; the effective date above identifies this version.
